The Science

Proven by
atomic clocks.

In 2010, physicists at NIST confirmed gravitational time dilation across just 33 centimeters — less than your forearm. This app shows you your personal piece of that experiment, happening right now.

Δt = (gh/c²) × t
g = gravitational acceleration (9.8 m/s²)
h = height difference (head to feet)
c = speed of light (299,792,458 m/s)
t = elapsed time
